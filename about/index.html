Hi, I'm Paul.

After studying Industrial Design in Eindhoven—the heart of Dutch innovation—I spent nearly a decade working in different roles in the banking industry, ranging from software developer to UX-designer and from business engineer to personalisation marketeer. I truly liked what I did, but it always felt like something was missing.

A corporate reorganization at the bank last year was the wake-up call I didn't know I needed. With my role on the line, I finally had the space to consider what I actually wanted to do—and where my heart and ambition truly belonged.

I realized my real strength isn’t in doing one specific thing—it’s in the ability to switch perspectives and connecting the dots. It’s the "hunger" to create things that bridge the gap between the digital and the physical, between the code and the feel of an object.

That is why I created Watt IV.

The Mission

The mission is simple:
Putting the funk back in functionality.


We exist to end the era of the boring tool. We believe technology should spark curiosity, not noise. By merging the precision of engineering with the playfulness of design, we create the objects we wish we had as kids—tools that work perfectly, but have a soul you can feel.

Watt IV?

Inspired by the question we asked way too often as kids, but rarely do as adults. Watt IV (pronounced 'what if') is a reminder to keep asking 'what if' and to never stop creating and always staying curious.

We live in an age where anything is possible, especially with the rise of AI. But as we move forward, we have to be careful not to lose the essence of what makes us human. I believe technology should enhance our daily lives, not take away from them.

The Weekly Drop

Starting March 1st, every Monday at 8:00 AM sharp (CET), I’ll be sharing something new. It might be a rough concept, a 3D-printed prototype, or a fully finished product with instructions to build your own.

For years, I’ve been a "sponge"—absorbing ideas from places like Twitter, Reddit and Hackaday, but always too hesitant to share my own work. Starting Watt IV is a giant leap for me, but it’s finally time to stop watching and start contributing.

I’m building the things I wish existed. I hope they bring a smile to your face, too.

Welcome to my mind.

Welcome to Watt IV.
