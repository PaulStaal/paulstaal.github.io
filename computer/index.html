the Vision

"the Computer" is a functional homage to the classic Lego computer brick, upscaled and re-imagined as a high-performance workstation. This project bridges the gap between play and productivity. It's not just a case; it's an enchanted housing that combines the aesthetics and charm of the Macintosh, the playfulness and modularity of the LEGO system, and the power of modern silicon. It demands intention, rewards curiosity, and turns every desktop interaction into a moment of tactile satisfaction.

But how did we get here?

the Magic

Modeled from scratch in Fusion 360, "the Computer" was engineered for accessibility and modularity. The design consists of multiple 3D-printable parts, allowing anyone to manufacture and assemble their own unit with standard hardware like heat-set inserts and screws.

The challenge was in the integration: fitting a full M4 Mac Mini, a 7-inch display, and an array of peripherals into a form factor that remains true to the original brick. Every detail was considered, from the "pocket" in the back that acts as a handle (a nod to the original Mac) to the fold-out "wings" that manage the power cable with classic elegance.

the Manifest

In the true spirit of the maker community, "the Computer" is designed to be shared. By utilizing 3D printing and minimal off-the-shelf hardware, we've created a premium desktop experience that prioritizes the "how it's made" as much as the final aesthetic.
